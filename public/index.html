Store Information

About Us

Mellisa Store is a family-owned sari-sari and general merchandise store serving our neighbors in Bacungan, Magsaysay.

Our store started small in September 2009, opening with only a handful of everyday goods for the neighborhood. Over the years the store grew in size and capital, letting us carry more products and offer more to the community.

That steady growth also meant we could take on workers, so today a small team helps run the store day to day — stocking shelves, serving customers, and keeping the tindahan open for our neighbors.
